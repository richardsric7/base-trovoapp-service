Our Products - Trovo App

A decentralized, non-custodial platform for asset tokenization, Trovo App is the primary gateway for tokenized securities. It features multi-signature wallets, multi-sub-wallets, and a range of advanced features for the secure storage and exchange of digital assets.

Our Products - Trovo P2P

A market infrastructure for the decentralized, non-custodial, and peer-to-peer trading of digital assets. Equipped with a platform-managed escrow service, TrovoP2P serves as a secondary market for tokenized assets, ensuring safe and secure transactions on a global scale.
