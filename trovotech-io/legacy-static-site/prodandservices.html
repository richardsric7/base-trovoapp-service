Problems Trovotech is solving

Trovotech intends to solve practical problems with a suite of products and services it is developing and will develop in the future. Some of the problems being addressed with our suite of products are as follows;

  • LOSS OF ASSETS DUE TO MISPLACED WALLET SECRET KEY

    Problem: On the Bantu Blockchain and on most other blockchains there is no way to regain access to a wallet when the secret key or passphrase is lost, this is a major challenge that still keeps many people from using blockchain wallets.

    Solution: Trovo App is one of the first wallets that has a secure and seamless wallet recovery process for users who have lost their secret keys.

  • POOR BLOCKCHAIN WALLET USER EXPERIENCE FOR BUSINESSES AND CORPORATES

    Problem: Blockchain wallets are typically designed for the individual user and this design approach makes it difficult for Corporate users to maintain their governance structure and approval levels for payments and other transactions on these blockchain-based business wallets. Anyone with the wallet secret key can solely carry out transactions on the wallet, therefore putting the company's funds at risk of theft.

    Solution: Trovotech provides a solution to this issue by developing a feature that makes it possible for any organization to retain its approval process while using the wallet, therefore, no single person can solely carry out a transaction or move the funds from the wallet without the due approval of all the authorized persons in the organization.

  • MANAGING MULTIPLE WALLETS ON THE SAME NETWORK IN A SINGLE DEVICE

    Problem: On the Bantu Blockchain, there is presently no way of managing multiple wallets except by separately and inefficiently registering each instance of every wallet a user wants to have, usually on separate devices.

    Solution: The Trovo App allows users to download their other Wallets in the Bantu ecosystem as sub-wallets for ease of custody and management of their assets.

  • RETAIL PAYMENTS USING BLOCKCHAIN

    Problem: Using blockchain for payment settlements solves problems such as unwarranted multiple debits due to system or network failures, failed transactions, delayed settlements, erratic chargebacks, and other related issues. Using blockchain wallets to pay at shops will significantly solve these challenges but there are no solution providers currently taking this challenge head on using blockchain.

    Solution: Trovotech is tackling this problem with its blockchain wallet. The Trovo App allows shop owners to safely and reliably receive payments at their Points of Sale while eliminating the challenges previously mentioned.

  • TOKENIZATION OF REAL WORLD ASSETS

    Problem: There is a lot of liquidity trapped in real world assets due to the real world assets such as high brow real estate and property, natural resources and even commodities. Many people are priced out of participating in these markets thus disenfranchising them from the inherent economic opportunities and rewards. Asset owners and managers are also not able to readily liquidate these assets because of the small percentage of investors that can afford to play in the space.

    Solution: With the Asset Tokenization feature on the Trovo App, these asset owners can now digitally fractionalise their assets and tokenize these fragments on the blockchain, thus making them available to a wider market and reducing the cost barrier to entry. This reduced friction means that more people with less funds can now partake in this market, the market is more liquid, and developers can easily raise funds to execute projects. In addition, since the price of natural resources and commodities are often more stable than many currencies, tokenized natural resources will ensure that people can hedge against inflation and transact using more relatively stable assets.

  • Problem: There is no existing NFT marketplace and by extension no NFT wallet in the Bantu Blockchain ecosystem.

    Solution: Trovo App is the first and only wallet that can custody NFTs on the Bantu Blockchain. Users can now mint, list, and trade NFTs on the Bantu Blockchain.

  • TICKETS AS NFTs

    Problem: With the traditional method of issuing tickets for events and activities, there is no reliable way of preserving used tickets for future value derivation like loyalty rewards, or possible sale on the secondary market. People who have sentimental attachments to past events they attended cannot carry their physical ticket mementos around.   18. Asset Tokenization

Real asset tokenization is an emerging profitable trend representing the convergence of investing and blockchain technology. Tokenization helps assets or fund owners raise capital more efficiently, and gives investors unprecedented access to private real asset investments, transparency, and liquidity. Tokenization is a method that converts rights to an asset into a digital token, in many ways similar to the traditional process of securitization. Transferring the information from a real-world asset onto the blockchain allows ownership rights to be transmitted and traded on a global and secure digital platform.

The foundation of a token economy offers the potential for a more efficient and fair financial inclusiveness by greatly reducing the friction involved in the creation, buying, and selling of assets. Features like integrity, robustness, accessibility, and immutability make blockchain a powerful accounting tool and the process of asset tokenization creates a myriad of advantages, including greater transparency, liquidity, and accessibility as well as faster and cheaper transactions.

A Real Estate asset token for example is capable of having the token holder’s rights and legal responsibilities embedded directly into the token, along with an immutable record of ownership. This immutable record means that nobody can “erase” your ownership even if it is not registered in a government-run registry. These characteristics promise to add transparency by tracking and recording the history of the asset every single time it changes hands.

Importantly, tokenization will open up investment in assets to a much wider audience - thanks to reduced minimum investment amounts and periods. Tokens are highly divisible - meaning investors can purchase tokens that represent small percentages of the underlying asset. If each order is cheaper and easier to process, it will open the way to a significant reduction of minimum investment amounts. Moreover, the higher liquidity of asset-backed tokens could reduce minimum investment periods, since investors can exchange their tokens on secondary markets which theoretically are global and open 24/7 (subject to regulatory limits).

The Trovo App has a built-in asset tokenization platform.

NFT Marketplace (TrovoNFT and TrovoTix)

NFT stands for Non-Fungible Token. They are unique digital tokens that represent real-world objects like art, photos, music, videos, in-game items, domain names, creative writing, certificates, tickets, etc. NFTs have original identification codes and metadata that make each NFT unique. 

Their uniqueness makes it easy to verify and validate their ownership. Transfers between owners can also be verified and validated.

NFTs have been mostly leveraged by digital content creators to guarantee the ownership of their creative content, while it also makes it possible for creators to directly realize profits from their work by selling their creative items as NFTs directly to consumers on an NFT marketplace, instead of relying on stores, galleries or auctions houses to sell these items. Royalties for selling these items to new owners can also go directly to the original creator whenever a sale takes place.

However, there are so many other applications where NFTs play a huge role beyond digital arts and games as we see today. For example, NFTs can be used in real estate, Intellectual property and patents, supply chain, and product authentication. ticketing, academic records, medical records, identity verification, etc,

The value that can be derived from NFTs is enormous and for this reason, Trovotech is building an NFT marketplace where the use cases of the NFT can be showcased and utilized.
