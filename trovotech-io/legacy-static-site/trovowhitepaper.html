Trovo Token - TROV

A utility token is a special kind of blockchain digital asset that gives its holders access to the services provided by the utility token issuer. It is one of the ways businesses reinvent themselves to ensure that their businesses keep up with the fast-paced world of web 3 technology and also ensure that their customers receive more value from using their products and services.

Trovotech is building an ecosystem of trust and we want to make it possible for our ecosystem users to have easy value-added access to our suite of products and services both now and in the future.

TROV utility token is a token issued on the Bantu Blockchain protocol, it was created to ensure that the ecosystem supporters and users derive maximum benefit from the products and services available in the Trovotech ecosystem. 

We chose Bantu Blockchain for TROV token issuance because of its many benefits. Amongst other things, Bantu’s purpose is to create an innovative value exchange platform that focuses on empowering African communities in a peer-to-peer manner.

There are many blockchain protocols out there with various characteristics, one of the main determinant factors for Bantu Blockchain being the blockchain of choice for Trovotech is the consensus mechanism, consensus mechanism is simply a system that allows all the computers in a blockchain network, called nodes to agree on which transactions are valid, it is this process that is used in verifying and confirming new transactions and also adding them to the blockchain. 

There are two major consensus mechanisms that are popularly used today in the blockchain space namely; proof of work and proof of stake consensus mechanisms.

Proof of work consensus mechanism is a computational way to verify the accuracy of transactions on the network, where a number of people across the world with computers called miners are required to use an enormous amount of computational power to solve a mathematical puzzle in order to confirm a transaction and update the decentralized ledger which exists on each of the miners globally. The miner that solves this mathematical puzzle also earns the reward of new tokens which are released by the network whenever new blocks are mined. This method is a proven, robust way of maintaining a secure decentralized blockchain, however, it consumes a lot of power, takes a lot of time, and greatly reduces the throughput of transactions, thereby making transactions very slow and expensive, so it does not give the scalability and speed that we need in our ecosystem. Bitcoin and Ethereum use the proof of work consensus mechanism.

Proof of stake consensus mechanism tries to improve on the speed and cost issue that the proof of work blockchains have. In a proof of stake system, staking plays a similar role to mining in proof of work, where a network participant gets selected to add the latest batch of transactions to the blockchain and earn some new tokens in exchange. The network relies on a group of participants called validators who contribute or stake their own digital assets, in exchange for a chance to validate incoming transactions and add them as new blocks on the chain.

The method of selecting the winner here is based on the number of assets each validator has in the staking pool, and the age of the locked asset, the validators that are most invested usually get rewarded. Once the winner has validated the latest block of transactions, other validators will confirm that the block is correct and when a certain number or threshold of confirmations is achieved the blockchain is updated by the network. the network will distribute rewards to the participating validators based on the proportion of their stake.

It is a great responsibility to become a validator and requires some level of technical knowledge, a significant amount of the network token is also required to be locked up, so not everyone can become a validator, however, people tend to join staking pools run by others who have the technical knowledge. Solana, Cardano, and Tron are some blockchains with proof of stack consensus mechanism.

Trovotech services require that transactions are done within a few seconds, which means, it has to be on a Blockchain where consensus must be reached very fast while the accuracy and security of the network are maintained.

The Proof of work and proof of stake consensus mechanisms as described above still have their drawbacks such as; cost of power for computation, cost of transaction, speed, scalability, throughput, etc., in the case of proof of work, and vulnerability to different kinds of attacks such as low-cost bribe attacks, cost of becoming a validator, validators holding a large amount of the blockchain tokens and having undue influence on the network in the case of proof of stake blockchain.

Generally, the speed of transactions and the throughput of these two consensus mechanisms cannot be compared to what is provided by the Bantu Blockchain.

The Bantu Blockchain runs a totally different consensus mechanism called the Harambee Byzantine Agreement HBA based on the Stellar Consensus Protocol. It also embeds several incentives and rewards for its ecosystem users.

Harambee Byzantine Agreement - HBA has very low computing and financial requirements It lowers the barrier to entry and opens up financial systems to new participants.

Consensus is reached once a set of nodes usually called quorum reaches an agreement which makes it very fast.

This is a system where a group of nodes that trust one another can decide to trust other nodes, thereby forming an entire network of peers that trust one another mutually through other trusted peers. New entities can decide to set up a node, however, the entity will have to be trusted by other entities in order to be accepted in the network.

Quorum is determined in a decentralized way, by each node selecting what is called quorum slices. A quorum is a set of nodes sufficient to reach an agreement. A quorum slice is the subset of a quorum convincing one particular node of agreement. A quorum slice may be smaller than a quorum.

Each node communicates with one another and accepts verification and reaches consensus every 2–5 seconds with other trusted peers.

There is no requirement for costly power nor holding a huge amount of the blockchain token for you to be a validator on the HBA consensus protocol.

The following key properties of the Harambee Byzantine Agreement make it desirable for our products and services.

Decentralized control: Anyone is able to participate and run validator nodes, so no central authority dictates whose approval is required for consensus.

Low latency In practice, nodes can reach consensus at timescales humans expect for web or payment transactions, which is typically a few seconds at most.

Flexible trust: Users have the freedom to trust any combination of parties they see fit. For example, a small non-profit may play a key role in keeping much larger institutions honest.

Asymptotic security: Safety rests on digital signatures and hash families whose parameters can realistically be tuned to protect against adversaries with unimaginably vast computing power.

What is Trov Token used for?

The TROV token was created to be used to access various services and benefits on the Trovotech ecosystem such as;

  • Transaction Fees; 

TROV token will be used to pay transaction fees on Trovotech’s products and services. Users can acquire the tokens within the ecosystem to use in making fee payments whenever they carry out transactions.

  • Loyalty Rewards; 

TROV token will be used to reward loyal users, for various activities carried out in the ecosystem. There will be a reward system that monitors users' activities and rewards them accordingly based on the value added to the ecosystem from their usage of our various products and services in those markets.

  • Performance Incentives

TROV token will be used to reward top performers of the trovotech ecosystem as monitored and captured by our performance monitoring and reward system.

  • Creators and Creativity Reward

TROV token will be used to reward those who are creating solutions or businesses that further expand the TROV ecosystem. Some of the solutions include tokenized assets, NFTs, etc.

  • User Activness Incentives

The ecosystem needs to remain active with value-adding activities, therefore 20% of all TROV tokens consumed within the ecosystem as fees for any product or service after all commissions have been paid will be distributed to those who keep the ecosystem active based on their level of activity. Additional incentives can be applied from the community token allocation.

  • Value Transfer

All users of the Trovotech ecosystem can use the TROV token for value transfer within the Trovotech ecosystem, this value transfer can also be done outside of the trovotech ecosystem as we get integrated into other ecosystems outside of the Trovotech ecosystem.

  • Trading

TROV tokens will be available for exchange on the various secondary markets so that people can also easily acquire them for use from those markets.

  • Staking Services

Having the TROV token will give a user the eligibility to participate in various Trovotech staking reward programs that will be implemented within the ecosystem.

  • Voting Rights

Holders of the TROV token will be able to vote on any matter that requires voting in the Trovotech ecosystem. For example, if a certain feature is requested by the community to be implemented, it can be decided through community voting to feel the pulse of the community on the feature request;.. this is giving power to our users. 

  • Voting Incentive

Not only will users be able to participate in voting, they will also be compensated with the TROV token for participating in voting.

  • Advert Sharing Incentive

Trovo App will be used for a decentralized marketing product and participants in these marketing campaigns will be rewarded automatically using the TROV token.

  • Other Use Cases

There are other use cases that TROV token can fit into, e.g, lending services, liquidity farming, and other DEFI services. As we expand the Trovotech ecosystem, other use cases will continue to emerge.

Distribution of TROV Token

Trovotech has a subscription program for our products and services which gives special rights and privileges to our paid users, our community of users who have been supporting our business from its inception will be appreciated for their support, therefore, the first set of ecosystem users to receive the TROV will be the members of the Trovo ecosystem who have an active subscription on their accounts. 25% of the total supply of TROV tokens will be distributed among our paid members over a limited time frame.

The method for the distribution will be based on the category of membership, type of subscription, and the number of eligible members who come in within the available window. 

The number of tokens received will continue to be reduced as the distribution progresses until the last person receives the distribution within the specified distribution period.

There will also be a systematic distribution of the TROV token to our ecosystem users over a period of time, based on the use cases mentioned above. The more the ecosystem is used, the more TROV is distributed to those who are actively using the ecosystem based on their contribution to the growth of the ecosystem. The utility of the ecosystem becomes a way of mining the TROV token.

Voting with TROV Token<

This is one of the important and key uses of the TROV token within the Trovotech ecosystem.

Many organizations and institutions are looking for a reliable way of carrying out transparent, free, and fair voting. Blockchain technology comes to the rescue as an immutable, transparent, and secure system. Voting exercises done on the Blockchain will provide an undisputable and instant result that can be publicly available to all stakeholders.

Trovotech will leverage its token and wallet as a proof of concept for blockchain-based voting that can be adopted by other organizations and institutions.

For Trovotech ecosystem voting, We will be giving our community of users the power to decide on certain improvements to be carried out in the Trovotech ecosystem, for example, proposals may be put up from time to time to have a particular feature or a product or solution implemented, it may be put up for community voting to determine the level of acceptance or consensus towards such feature/product requests.

  • Trovotech Ecosystem Voting Method

To be eligible for voting in the ecosystem, a user must have up to 5 TROV tokens as a reserve in his/her wallet to be able to vote, this means that a user will be allowed to vote only if, after voting, his wallet balance remains 5 TROV or more.

Voting is done using 0.05 TROV tokens (this will be subject to change depending on the price of TROV tokens in the market).

Voting can be between two choices or multiple choices. To vote, a user only needs to make his/her choice among the items that are up for voting and confirm his/her choice, this action takes 0.05 TROV from his/her wallet.

  • Trovotech Ecosystem Voting Incentive

At the end of the voting exercise, All the TROV tokens used for the voting will be burnt, however, the user who participated in the voting exercise will receive the voting incentive from the community allocation of the TROV token. 

We are starting with the voting use case within the Trovotech ecosystem and any party or entity is welcome to carry out their voting exercise using the Trovo App. However, to spread it outside the Trovotech ecosystem, Trovotech is willing to enter into discussion with any entity, institution, or organization that would want to implement this system of voting via Blockchain. We are willing to provide the solution according to the party’s needs and will advise on the best way of implementation based on the agreed requirements.
